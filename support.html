Support

Need help with Pocket Playbox? Email us at support@wealthboardapp.com and we'll get back to you, usually within two working days. Telling us your phone model and which game you were playing helps us fix things faster.

Common questions

Do I need the internet to play?

No. Every game can be played offline. You only need a connection for 1 vs 1 online matches, ads and buying tokens.

How does 1 vs 1 work?

Tap 1 vs 1 in a game that supports it, then use quick match or share a private code with a friend. Turn games (like Connect Four) take turns on one board. Score and finish games give you both the same board at the same time: the higher score, or whoever finishes first, wins.

What are tokens?

Tokens pay for extras in some games. You can get free tokens by watching a short video, or buy a pack in the shop.

I bought tokens but didn't get them.

Purchases go through the App Store or Google Play. Close and reopen the app, check you're signed in with the same store account, then use Restore purchases in the shop if it's shown. If they still haven't arrived, email us with your purchase receipt.

Spelling Bee doesn't speak the words.

Spelling Bee uses your phone's built-in voice. Turn the volume up and, on iPhone, check the silent switch. On Android, make sure a text-to-speech engine is installed (Settings › Accessibility › Text-to-speech output). If no voice is available, tap the speaker button to peek at the word instead.

How do I turn sounds or music off?

Open Settings in the app and switch off Sound effects or Music.

How do I see the how-to-play again?

Tap the ? button at the top of any game, or use Settings › Show how-to-play again.
