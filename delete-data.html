Delete my data

Pocket Playbox by Wealthboard Apps

Pocket Playbox doesn't use accounts. Most of your information lives only on your phone, and the rest is the record of online 1 vs 1 matches on our game server.

1. Delete what's on your phone

Delete the app. That removes your scores, tokens, progress and settings. Tokens can't be recovered once the app is deleted, unless they were purchases that the store can restore.

2. Delete your online data (in the app)

Open Pocket Playbox, go to Settings › Your data › Delete my online data and tap Delete. The app deletes your online profile from our server straight away and gives your phone a fresh one. Your scores, tokens and purchases on the phone are not affected.

Or ask us by email

If you no longer have the app, email support@wealthboardapp.com with the subject "Delete my Pocket Playbox data" and the player name you used online (and your player ID if you have it). We'll delete it within 30 days and let you know.

What gets deleted

  • Your player name, player ID and friend code
  • Your friends list, friend requests, invites and blocks
  • Your online match history and scores

What we may keep

Nothing that identifies you. Purchase records are kept by Apple or Google, not by us. Ad networks keep their own data; you can reset your advertising ID in your phone's settings.
