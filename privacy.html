Privacy policy

Pocket Playbox · Last updated 17 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Pocket Playbox app ("the app") uses, why, and the choices you have. The app is made by Wealthboard Apps ("we", "us"). If you have questions, email support@wealthboardapp.com.

The short version

  • You don't need an account, and we don't ask for your name, email, phone number or location.
  • Your scores, tokens and settings are stored on your own device.
  • If you play 1 vs 1 online, our game server receives the player name you choose and your match moves and scores.
  • The app shows ads through Unity LevelPlay, which may use your device's advertising ID.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple or Google; we never see your payment details.

Information stored on your device

Game progress, best scores, tokens, unlocked items and settings (such as sound on or off) are saved in the app's storage on your phone. This information stays on your device and is removed when you delete the app.

Online 1 vs 1 play

Online play is optional. When you use it, the app connects to our game server (api.wordglint.wealthboardapp.com) and sends: the player name you pick (it can be anything; please don't use your real full name), a random player ID created by the app, the match code, and your moves or scores during the match. We use this only to run matches and show results to you and your opponent. Match records are kept for up to 30 days and then deleted. Your player name is shown to the people you play against.

Advertising

The app shows ads using Unity LevelPlay and the ad networks it works with. To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, these companies may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iPhone, Advertising ID on Android), IP address, device model, operating system and how you interact with ads. On iPhone, ads are only personalised if you allow tracking when asked; you can change this any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. On Android you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in Settings › Google › Ads. See Unity's privacy policy for details of how they use this information.

Purchases

Token packs are bought through the Apple App Store or Google Play. They process your payment under their own privacy policies and tell the app only whether a purchase succeeded.

What we don't do

We don't sell your personal information, we don't ask for access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos or location, and we don't use your information for anything other than running the app and its ads.

Children

Pocket Playbox is a general-audience app and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

Deleting your information

You can delete everything stored on your device by deleting the app. To have your online play records removed from our server, follow the steps on Delete my data.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example in the EU, UK or California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information about you, or to object to how it's used. Email us and we'll help; we may need your player name or match code to find your records.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update this page and the date at the top. Big changes will also be mentioned in the app's update notes.
